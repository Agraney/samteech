Operational Metric Workbook Source Sheet Excel Rows / Columns Calculation Logic
Total Stock Value STOCK MASTER Columns: Closing Balance × Unit Rate Sum of closing_balance × rate across all 72 tracked materials in snapshot.
Critical Stock Alerts STOCK MASTER Columns: Closing Balance, Min Level Count of materials where closing_balance ≤ 0 (out of stock) or run-out ≤ 7 days.
Production Output PROD_HV WINDING to PROD_PAINTING Rows 3–367 (Daily Units) Sum of daily production output (excluding summary row 369 to avoid double-counting).
Dispatch Output PROD_DISPATCH Rows 3–367 (Daily Units) Customer shipments tracked separately from shop-floor manufacturing stages.
Testing Pass Rate PROD_TESTING PASSED & FAILED Rows 3–367 (Units) Passed / (Passed + Failed) × 100%.
Physical WIP Buffers STK_*_FINISHED TR, CCA FIN, HV COIL, LV COIL Closing Balances in Stock Ledgers Warehouse inventory of sub-assemblies waiting for downstream assembly.
Stage Accumulation / WIP PROD_* section outputs Period output delta: Stage A − Stage B Units completed upstream but not yet finished downstream during the period.
Monthly Targets & Variance PRODUCTION MASTER Columns: Apr-26 to Mar-27 Target, Actual, and Variance units across all 7 sections and 5 ratings.
Material Readiness CONSUMPTION / BASIC & STOCK MASTER Norms × Planned Units vs Closing Stock Gap = (Norm × Planned) − Available Stock. Status = Healthy / Attention / Insufficient.
In-House vs Outsourced STK_*_FINISHED TR vs STK_*_OUTSOURCED TR Closing Balances in finished ledgers Ratio of in-house manufactured vs externally purchased units.
